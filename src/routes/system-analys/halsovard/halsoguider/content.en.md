# Health guides

[← Back to the main article](..)

**Summary:** A shorter training course via folk high schools and study associations for people who are to work preventively with lifestyle habits and guide people to healthcare, associational life and municipal services. The health guide is not a healthcare profession and does not give medical advice, but works in close cooperation with healthcare.

*This is a proposal to test in limited pilots with evaluation. The figures below are proposals and rough estimates, not results.*

## Why

Many people have questions about diet, exercise, sleep and stress that do not require a doctor, but that can still be difficult to get answers to. Others find it difficult to navigate healthcare or do not know which associations and activities exist. This applies in particular to people who are new in Sweden, older people who do not use digital services and people with limited resources.

## What already exists

- **Health communicators** inform newly arrived people about health and how healthcare works in several places in the country, in their own language.
- **Link workers** in the United Kingdom, so-called social prescribing link workers, are an established role in primary care that guides patients to activities and support.
- **Primary care’s own staff**, such as district nurses and physiotherapists, already work with lifestyle habits.

## How it would work

**Training (proposal: a few weeks, with practical placement):**
- basic knowledge about lifestyle habits and health
- motivational interviewing
- knowledge about healthcare, municipal services and local associations
- boundaries: when to refer to healthcare, confidentiality and ethics

**Tasks:**
- conversations and group meetings about lifestyle habits
- help to navigate healthcare, for example with 1177
- guiding people to associations, study associations and activities
- outreach information in residential areas, at libraries and in associations

**Employment:** in the municipality, in a housing company, at a health centre or in a non-profit organisation, with access to supervision from licensed staff.

## Pilot proposal

**Scope:** Three municipalities of different types over three years, with four to six health guides per municipality, in cooperation with the region’s primary care.

**Evaluation:** number and type of contacts, who is reached, participants’ experience, and changes in healthcare contacts and lifestyle habits compared with a comparison area.

## Rough cost estimate

| Item | Cost per municipality and year |
|------|---------------------------|
| Salaries (4 health guides at half-time) | approx. SEK 0.7–0.9 million |
| Training and supervision | approx. SEK 0.2 million |
| Evaluation | approx. SEK 0.1 million |

## Legal and practical issues

1. **Boundary with health and medical care:** Health guides do not provide health and medical care. The boundary needs to be clear in the training and in the work.
2. **Confidentiality:** A health guide employed by the municipality is covered by the Public Access to Information and Secrecy Act. For employees in non-profit organisations, confidentiality is needed through agreement.
3. **Training standard:** If the role spreads, a common training standard is needed. Whether the professional title should be protected, as that of assistant nurses, is a later question.

## Objections

**“More nurses are needed, not laypeople.”** Health guides do not replace healthcare staff. If the role turns out to take resources from healthcare instead of complementing it, that is a reason not to proceed.

**“Who is responsible if the guide gives wrong advice?”** The guide should not give medical advice and should always refer to healthcare in case of symptoms. Supervision from licensed staff is needed.

**“There are already health communicators.”** Correct, and the proposal should build on their experiences instead of creating a parallel role.

## What you can do

**If you are a municipal politician:** Find out whether the municipality or region has health communicators, and what experience they have.

**If you work in popular education:** Investigate whether there is interest from the municipality and region in a joint training course.
