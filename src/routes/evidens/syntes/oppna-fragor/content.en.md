# Open Questions

## The forward-looking research agenda

Questions here may later become hypotheses, studies, simulations, or experiments. This is the most honest single page in the Evidence section about what the project doesn't yet know — see **[What We Don't Know](/evidens/syntes/okanda/)** for the underlying evidence gaps these questions respond to.

---

## From the current corpus

- Which governance functions have the strongest scale effects, and which have essentially none? (Relates to R2, planned.)
- How much local information actually survives institutional representation chains — and can this be measured directly rather than inferred? (Relates to H10, the [S1 case](/evidens/fall/sverige/kommunsammanslagningar/).)
- When does intermunicipal cooperation outperform merger, for which specific functions, and by how much? (Relates to H8, evidence gap #3.)
- When does formal decentralization produce nested centralization instead of substantive subsidiarity, and what early indicators would reveal this before outcomes are observed? (Relates to H4.)
- What institutional architectures preserve plural observation without simply adding coordination cost? (Relates to H13, currently GAE-derived only.)
- How do governance systems learn from local experiments — and how much of Sweden's 1979 "local organs" correction actually improved outcomes, versus simply changing formal structure again? (Relates to H15.)

---

## From R1 specifically

- Does the Bolivia-style "decentralization tracks local need" pattern (Faguet 2004) replicate in Nordic or other high-capacity administrative contexts, or is it specific to contexts with larger initial information gaps?
- What would a direct measure of "decision proximity" (as opposed to jurisdiction size) actually look like for Swedish municipalities?

## From R8 (civic capability) specifically

- Do the knowledge and efficacy gains found in international mini-public research (see the 2025 meta-analysis referenced in [What We Don't Know](/evidens/syntes/okanda/)) replicate in a Swedish municipal or regional deliberative process?
- Is there a validated, independent way to measure "systems reasoning" that doesn't risk becoming self-confirming? If not, should this construct be dropped from the corpus rather than measured poorly?
- What would distinguish institutions that build civic capability from institutions that merely assume it exists?

---

## Structural / cross-cutting questions

- What is the right way to aggregate evidence across cases with different causal identification strength, time horizons, and definitions of decentralization, without producing a premature "subsidiarity score"? (See the explicit methodological warning in the underlying case corpus.)
- How should the corpus represent co-evolution between architecture and capability (H18, H20) rather than reducing everything to a one-directional "education → better governance" claim?
- Which of the 29 GAE reports have the strongest, most directly testable institutional interpretations — i.e., where should the [How We Know → GAE lens](/evidens/sa-vet-vi/gae/) mapping be expanded first?
