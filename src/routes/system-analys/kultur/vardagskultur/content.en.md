# Grants for everyday culture

[← Back to the main article](..)

**Summary:** Small grants, proposed at SEK 5,000, for people who want to carry out a cultural project without being professionally active: writing down local history, recording music, arranging a storytelling evening, documenting a tradition or a subculture. The application should be simple and possible to make in different ways, and the assessment should be local.

*This is a proposal to test in limited pilots with evaluation. The figures below are proposals and rough estimates, not results.*

## Why

A great deal of cultural creation takes place outside institutions: in associations, homes, online and in neighbourhoods. Most public support is aimed at professional cultural creators or at associations, and often requires experience in writing applications. This can make it difficult for people who have never had contact with the cultural support system to receive support, even for small projects.

## How it would work

**The grant:**
- maximum SEK 5,000 per person per year
- no requirements for previous merits, education or membership
- can be used for materials, equipment, room rent or printing

**The application:**
- a short text, audio recording or video, in which the applicant explains what they want to do and what the money is needed for
- the possibility of getting help with the application at a library or in an association
- the application can be made in languages other than Swedish

**Assessment:**
- a local group, for example with representatives from associational life and previous recipients, assesses whether the application is feasible and serious, not its artistic quality
- reply within one month

**Reporting:** a simple report afterwards, for example a picture, a recording or a short text about what was done.

## Pilot proposal

**Scope:** Ten municipalities of different types over two years, with, for example, SEK 1 million per municipality per year, that is around 200 grants per municipality.

**Evaluation:** how many apply, how many have never previously received cultural support, which groups are reached and not reached, what was carried out and how recipients experienced the process.

## Rough cost estimate

| Item | Cost per year |
|------|--------------|
| Grants (10 municipalities × SEK 1 million) | SEK 10 million |
| Administration and support for applicants | approx. SEK 2 million |
| Evaluation | approx. SEK 1 million |
| **Total** | **approx. SEK 13 million** |

Municipalities can also test the grant on their own within their existing cultural budgets.

## Legal and practical issues

1. **Administrative law:** Grants from an authority or municipality must be handled according to administrative law principles of equal treatment and documentation, even when the application is simple.
2. **Personal data:** Videos and audio recordings contain personal data and must be handled in accordance with data protection rules.
3. **Tax:** How the grant is to be taxed needs to be clarified, so that recipients are not taken by surprise.

## Objections

**“The money goes to activities of low quality.”** The purpose is for more people to be able to create, not to select the best. Professional culture has its own forms of support.

**“It will lead to fraud.”** The amounts are small, and reporting afterwards plus spot checks should reduce the risk.

**“Those who are already active take the money.”** The evaluation should monitor who applies. Outreach information via libraries, schools and associations may be needed to reach others.

## Experience to build on

- **Kulturbryggan**, which supports innovative cultural projects, with different conditions than traditional cultural support.
- **Municipal support for associations and organisers**, which exists in most municipalities.
- **Popular education**, where study associations already support amateur culture on a large scale, for example through study circles in music and creative work.

## What you can do

**If you are a municipal politician:** Propose a pilot with small, simple grants for everyday culture within the cultural budget.

**If you work in a cultural administration:** Review how easy it is to apply for the smallest grants in the municipality, and test a simplified application.

**If you are a cultural creator:** Contact a study association, which can often support even small projects.
