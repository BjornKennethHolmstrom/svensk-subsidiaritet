# Synthesis

## What we can say right now — and what we can't

Synthesis is where Research, Cases, and Data & Models are weighed together into a current state of knowledge. It's deliberately **provisional**: statuses reflect the current corpus, not final conclusions, and should be updated as new studies, cases, data and models are added.

---

## Four parts

### [The Evidence Matrix →](/evidens/syntes/matris/)
The main table: all 20 hypotheses (H1–H20), their current evidence, counterevidence, mechanism fit, confidence and status. This is the single most important synthesis page in the whole Evidence section.

### [What We Know →](/evidens/syntes/vad-vi-vet/)
Only conclusions the corpus actually supports, with clearly stated scope and confidence.

### [Counterevidence →](/evidens/syntes/motevidens/)
Not "the opposition" — a systematic review of which observations make our hypotheses less credible. Treated as a first-class page, not a text field at the bottom.

### [What We Don't Know →](/evidens/syntes/okanda/)
Explicitly documented knowledge gaps. Writing "we don't know this yet" is a feature, not a failure.

### [Open Questions →](/evidens/syntes/oppna-fragor/)
The active, forward-looking research agenda — what should be investigated next.

---

## Update rule

The matrix is versioned. Every significant status change records the date, what evidence was added, and the reason for the change. A hypothesis doesn't move from "unresolved" to "supported" just because one new supportive study appears, if meaningful counterevidence remains unexplored.
