# Research

## Nine empirical questions, not nine theoretical papers

The Research section is organized around **empirical questions**, not around however the theoretical GAE series happens to be structured. The same case or study can therefore appear under several topics — a municipal merger can be relevant to scale, capacity and democratic proximity all at once.

Each topic page is a **literature synthesis**, not a single study. It asks: *what has the research found so far on this question?* — and it doesn't hide disagreement. "Mixed evidence" is a fully legitimate conclusion.
