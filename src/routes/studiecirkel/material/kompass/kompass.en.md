## Three questions

The three questions are answered separately. A system can have decisions far away but still be robust, or have decisions close by but be vulnerable.

### 1. Decision – where are decisions made?

- Who decides the budget?
- Who decides the rules?
- Are different parts of the decisions made at different levels?

**Marking:** National/EU ☐☐☐☐☐☐☐☐☐☐ Local/close

### 2. Knowledge – whose knowledge is used?

- What knowledge does the system listen to: formal expertise, staff experience, users' knowledge?
- Is there knowledge that is lost?

**Marking:** Formal expertise ☐☐☐☐☐☐☐☐☐☐ Experience and local knowledge

### 3. Resilience – how does the system cope with disruptions?

- What happens if something stops working?
- Are there backup routes or alternatives?
- Who has the resources to cope with a major crisis?

**Marking:** Vulnerable ☐☐☐☐☐☐☐☐☐☐ Robust
