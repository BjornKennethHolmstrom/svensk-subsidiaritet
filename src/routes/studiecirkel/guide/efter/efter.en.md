# **After the circle – From conversation to society**

## **Closing rituals (for the Swedish soul)**

**The last meeting:**
1. **Go through the "Treasure Map"** – see what resources you actually have
2. **Choose ONE thing** to do within 30 days (small, concrete, possible)
3. **Write a "30-day promise"** that those who want to can sign
4. **Set a date for "Meeting 5: Follow-up"** (if you want)
5. **Celebrate with extra fika** (could be semlor, punsch, something festive)

**Template for 30-day promise:**
> "We, the participants in [Group name], promise to do the following before [Date]: [Concrete action]. We will return on [Date] to share what we have learned. Signed: [Signatures]"

## **Options for continued engagement**

### **Option 1: Continue as a study circle**
- **Theme:** "Subsidiarity for [your city/district]"
- **Focus:** Go deeper into one area (food, energy, care)
- **Resource:** Request special material from svensksubsidiaritet.se

### **Option 2: Start a practical project**
- **Food:** Buying club, allotment community, "eat together" evenings
- **Energy:** Solar panel group purchase, energy-saving club
- **Safety:** Night walks, neighbourhood watch together with the police, "walking school bus" for children
- **Support:** Pattern library at svensksubsidiaritet.se

### **Option 3: Become ambassadors**
- Invite the municipal commissioner/regional politician to a meeting
- Write a letter to the editor in the local newspaper
- Start a circle in the next neighbourhood/workplace

## **How you share your learning (so others can grow)**

1. **Go to svensksubsidiaritet.se**
2. **Click "Share your circle's experience"**
3. **Fill in a short form:**
   - Which area are you from?
   - Which system did you focus on?
   - What worked well?
   - What would you do differently?
   - Which action did you choose?

**Why this is important:** Your experiences become **patterns** in our library. The next group in Kiruna or Malmö can learn from you.

## **When it feels too big...**

**Remember:**
1. **No one expects you to solve the housing crisis.** Even a small joint initiative is worth something.
2. **Silence is okay.** The group might sleep for a while and wake up in the spring.
3. **You are not alone.** Many others are thinking about the same questions, and your experiences can help other groups.

## **The last word (to you as circle leader)**

Thank you. You have done something brave – you have said "maybe there is another way" and created a space for others to explore it together.

Whatever happens now: You have planted a seed. It might sprout immediately. It might lie in the ground over the winter. But you have broken the ice.

**Study circles have for over a hundred years been a way for people in Sweden to learn together and organise themselves. Now you have done it too.**
