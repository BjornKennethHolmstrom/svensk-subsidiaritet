# Research

## Nine empirical questions, not nine theoretical papers

The Research section is organized around **empirical questions**, not around however the theoretical GAE series happens to be structured. The same case or study can therefore appear under several topics — a municipal merger can be relevant to scale, capacity and democratic proximity all at once.

Each topic page is a **literature synthesis**, not a single study. It asks: *what has the research found so far on this question?* — and it doesn't hide disagreement. "Mixed evidence" is a fully legitimate conclusion.

All nine topics are now built, but at varying depth depending on how much evidence actually exists in the corpus — see the status markers below.

---

## The nine topics

### [R1 — Subsidiarity & Decentralization →](/evidens/forskning/subsidiaritet-decentralisering/)
When does decentralized decision-making improve the fit between problems, information, authority and action?
**Status: well-supported**

### [R2 — Scale, Capacity & Specialization →](/evidens/forskning/skala-kapacitet-specialisering/)
When does larger scale provide superior administrative capacity, efficiency or specialization?
**Status: well-supported**

### [R3 — Spillovers & Multilevel Governance →](/evidens/forskning/spridningseffekter-flernivastyrning/)
What happens when a problem's causes or effects cross institutional or geographic boundaries?
**Status: partial** — currently rests on a single case (S3)

### [R4 — Polycentric Governance →](/evidens/forskning/polycentrisk-styrning/)
When can multiple relatively autonomous decision centres outperform a single centre?
**Status: partial** — international commons cases still missing

### [R5 — Democratic Proximity & Participation →](/evidens/forskning/demokratisk-narhet-deltagande/)
When does proximity and participation improve democratic quality and decision-making?
**Status: partial** — dedicated participation cases are planned

### [R6 — Public Finance, Equalization & Risk Pooling →](/evidens/forskning/offentlig-finansiering-utjamning/)
How can local autonomy coexist with common financing, redistribution and risk sharing?
**Status: well-supported**

### [R7 — Coordination, Complexity & Accountability →](/evidens/forskning/samordning-komplexitet-ansvar/)
When does multilevel governance become so complex that coordination, accountability or decision speed deteriorate?
**Status: well-supported**

### [R8 — Civic Capability & Governance →](/evidens/forskning/medborgarkapacitet-styrning/)
Which knowledge, skills and forms of participation are required for decentralized and adaptive governance to work well?
**Status: exploratory** — the empirical corpus is still under development; see in particular why we don't use developmental-stage models as a scientific basis

### [R9 — Governance & Adaptation →](/evidens/forskning/styrning-anpassning/)
Which governance systems can detect, learn from and correct their own failures?
**Status: partial** — a pattern identified across three cases, but without outcome evidence yet

---

## On the current state

All nine topics now have a complete page, but **depth honestly varies with the evidence available**: R1, R2, R6 and R7 rest on multiple cases and are relatively well-supported; R3, R4, R5 and R9 rest on one or two cases and should be read as initial patterns; R8 is explicitly exploratory.
