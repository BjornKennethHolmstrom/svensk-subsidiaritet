# Our Questions

## The active research programme

Every question below is phrased neutrally — not as "prove decentralization works," but as a condition: **under what conditions does what hold?**

Each question can in turn link to hypotheses, research topics, cases, studies, models and open questions. The nine research topics below organize the entire evidence corpus.

---

## The nine research topics

### [R1 — Subsidiarity & Decentralization →](/evidens/forskning/subsidiaritet-decentralisering/)
> When does decentralized decision-making improve the fit between problems, information, authority and action?

### [R2 — Scale, Capacity & Specialization →](/evidens/forskning/skala-kapacitet-specialisering/)
> When does larger scale provide superior administrative capacity, efficiency or specialization?

Kept distinct from R1 — local information advantage and scale advantage are not the same mechanism.

### [R3 — Spillovers & Multilevel Governance →](/evidens/forskning/spridningseffekter-flernivastyrning/)
> What happens when a problem's causes, effects or dependencies cross institutional or geographic boundaries?

### [R4 — Polycentric Governance →](/evidens/forskning/polycentrisk-styrning/)
> When can multiple relatively autonomous decision centres outperform a single centre?

### [R5 — Democratic Proximity & Participation →](/evidens/forskning/demokratisk-narhet-deltagande/)
> When does proximity and participation improve democratic quality and decision-making?

Extends beyond turnout to include political efficacy, representation, accountability, deliberation, and participant learning.

### [R6 — Public Finance, Equalization & Risk Pooling →](/evidens/forskning/offentlig-finansiering-utjamning/)
> How can local autonomy coexist with common financing, redistribution and risk sharing?

The central question isn't whether financing should be centralized or decentralized, but which parts of the financial architecture need to operate at which scale.

### [R7 — Coordination, Complexity & Accountability →](/evidens/forskning/samordning-komplexitet-ansvar/)
> When does multilevel governance become so complex that coordination, accountability or decision speed deteriorate?

One of the main interfaces with GAE.

### [R8 — Civic Capability & Governance →](/evidens/forskning/medborgarkapacitet-styrning/)
> Which knowledge, skills, social capabilities and forms of participation are required for decentralized and adaptive governance to function well — and to what extent can institutions themselves develop those capabilities?

Marked **exploratory**: conceptually well-motivated, but the empirical corpus is still under development. See in particular the risks around using underdeveloped measures of "systems reasoning" or developmental stage.

### [R9 — Governance & Adaptation →](/evidens/forskning/styrning-anpassning/)
> Which governance systems can detect, learn from and correct their own failures?

The main domain where GAE's sequence **sense → learn → act** becomes directly relevant. Should be studied together with R8, since institutions' adaptive capacity may partly depend on the capabilities of the actors embedded in them.
