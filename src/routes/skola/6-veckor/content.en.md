# Systems thinking and subsidiarity: a 6-week module for upper secondary school

## This module is being revised

We have taken this module down temporarily. A review found factual errors in several of the case examples, and the structure steered students toward a set conclusion instead of letting them test the question themselves. That falls short of the requirements for objective and balanced teaching, and of the project's own [evidence standards](/evidens/sa-vet-vi/evidensstandarder).

If you have used the module before: the cases on pandemic management, Swiss healthcare, public transport and municipal finances should not be used in their current form.

A revised version is on its way, in which students meet arguments both for and against centralization and weigh the evidence themselves. In the meantime, the introductory lesson [Systems thinking in 60 minutes](/skola/60-minuter) can be used as it is.
