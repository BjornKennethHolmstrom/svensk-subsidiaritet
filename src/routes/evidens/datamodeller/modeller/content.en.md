# GAE Models

## The interpretive layer between our empirical questions and the GAE series

Governance as Engineering (GAE) already has 29 published reports and simulations on GitHub. This section doesn't duplicate them — it explains, for the reports most relevant to this project's hypotheses, **what the model tests, what it establishes within its own terms, and what it does not establish about real institutions.** Full technical treatment always lives in the original report.

See **[GAE as an Analytical Lens](/evidens/sa-vet-vi/gae/)** for the broader mapping and the [R]/[IP]/[H] system these pages use.

---

## Model spotlights

### [Requisite Variety (GAE IV) →](/evidens/datamodeller/modeller/nodvandig-variation/)
Why matching a governance system's internal variety to its problem environment's variety matters — the formal grounding for why local differentiation can be valuable rather than noise.

### [Coordination Tax (GAE V) →](/evidens/datamodeller/modeller/samordningsskatt/)
The structural cost of keeping multiple distributed decision-makers acting coherently — the counterweight to requisite variety, and central to **[R7](/evidens/forskning/samordning-komplexitet-ansvar/)**.

### [The Adaptation Bottleneck (GAE XV) →](/evidens/datamodeller/modeller/adaptationsflaskhalsen/)
Why a governance system's overall adaptive speed is limited by its slowest sense→learn→act link — directly relevant to **[R9](/evidens/forskning/styrning-anpassning/)**'s open question of why Swedish institutional corrections take years to decades.
